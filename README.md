## 🌍 The Problem

Despite rapid EV adoption, charging remains one of the biggest barriers to mass usage. EV users commonly face:

- 🔌 **Difficulty finding compatible charging stations**
- ❓ **Uncertainty** about station availability and reliability
- 🗺️ **Poor route planning** for long-distance travel
- 😰 **Range anxiety** caused by lack of real-time insights
- 🔄 **Inconsistent user experience** across charging networks

These issues lead to wasted time, stress, and reduced confidence in EV travel.

---

## 🚀 The ElectraPath™ Solution

ElectraPath™ acts as a smart intelligence layer between EV users and charging infrastructure. Instead of relying on scattered maps or incomplete data, users get a seamless, end-to-end charging journey experience.

The platform combines **station discovery, route-aware planning, real-time insights, and smooth session management** to ensure EV charging becomes a predictable and reliable part of everyday travel.

## 👤 User Features

### 🔐 Registration & Profile Management
- Simple and secure user onboarding
- Personal profile management
- Vehicle-specific configuration to ensure charger compatibility
- Personalized preferences for charging speed and station types

### 🗺️ Smart Route Planning *(Core Feature)*
- Route-based EV journey planning between source and destination
- Intelligent identification of optimal charging stops along the route
- Charging-aware travel planning to reduce range anxiety
- Clear visualization of reachable distance based on vehicle range

### 📍 Charging Station Discovery
- Interactive, map-based charging station discovery
- Real-time station visibility and operational status
- Advanced filtering by connector type, charging speed, and amenities
- Seamless navigation to selected charging stations

### ⏱️ Booking & Scheduling
- Advance reservation of charging slots
- Reduced waiting time at high-demand stations
- Digital queue awareness for better planning

### ⚡ Charging Session Management
- Seamless initiation of charging sessions
- Live session monitoring and progress tracking
- Clear visibility into charging status and remaining time
- Remote session control for enhanced convenience

### 💳 Payments & Billing
- Integrated in-app wallet for faster transactions
- Support for multiple digital payment methods
- Transparent billing and session history
- Downloadable invoices for completed sessions

### ⭐ Community & Feedback
- User ratings and reviews for charging stations
- Community-driven reliability insights
- Shared experiences to improve overall charging trust

## 🛠️ Admin & Operator Capabilities

### 📊 Dashboard & Analytics
- Centralized overview of platform activity
- Charging session insights and usage trends
- Demand patterns and operational metrics

### 🏗️ Station & Asset Management
- Add, edit, and manage charging station information
- Control charger availability and operational status
- Mark stations as offline or under maintenance
- Ensure accurate and verified station data

### 👥 User & Role Management
- Manage registered users and activity
- Support tools for issue resolution
- Role-based access control for sub-admin users

### 📅 Booking & Session Oversight
- Real-time visibility into active charging sessions
- Complete booking and session history
- Monitoring of cancellations and no-shows

### 💰 Pricing & Revenue Control
- Flexible pricing configuration
- Time-based and usage-based tariff management
- Promotional offers and discount control

### 🛠️ Maintenance & Support
- Automated alerts for station issues
- Ticketing system for user complaints
- Maintenance tracking and resolution workflows
